Project Vision

Build a secure, scalable platform that collects, manages, analyzes, and visualizes e-commerce business data, enabling business owners to make data-driven decisions through interactive dashboards, reports, and analytics.

🎯 End Goal

By the end of the project, you'll have a production-ready E-Commerce Business Intelligence Platform that demonstrates:

Secure backend development with Django REST Framework
Relational database design using MariaDB/MySQL
Role-based authentication with JWT
Data processing using Pandas and NumPy
Business analytics and KPI dashboards
AI-powered forecasting and recommendations
Modern React frontend
Containerized deployment with Docker
Cloud deployment and CI/CD practices

This roadmap gives you a cohesive progression where each phase builds the data and functionality needed for the next, culminating in a BI platform rather than just an online store.
